# Contributors

Thank you to everyone who made Selfhost-Remote-Mail-MCP better. Code is one way to help. Bug reports, ideas, translations, docs, design and answers in issues count the same.

Add yourself in your pull request, or ask in an issue and the maintainer adds you. Newest first.

| Who | What they did |
|---|---|
| [@Self-hosted-MCP](https://github.com/Self-hosted-MCP) | Created and maintains the project |

Kinds of contribution: code, bug reports, ideas, docs, translations, design, testing, reviews, answering questions, security reports, tutorials, talks.

Security researchers are credited in the published advisory and in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md), unless they prefer to stay anonymous.
